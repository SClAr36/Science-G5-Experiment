# Week 2 Science — Quick Review

## English

- **Testable question:** a question that can be answered with measurements in an experiment
- **Question pattern:** How does changing the **independent variable** affect the **dependent variable**?
- **Variable:** a factor that can change in an experiment
- **Independent variable:** what I change; the cause
- **Dependent variable:** what I measure or observe; the effect / outcome
- **Controlled variables / fair test:** keep other conditions the same and change only one factor
- **Density & buoyancy:** non-mixing liquids form layers; less dense → top, more dense → bottom

## 中文版

- **可检验问题：**能通过实验中的测量来回答的问题
- **提问句型：**改变**自变量**会怎样影响**因变量**？
- **变量：**实验中可能发生变化的因素
- **自变量：**主动改变的因素；原因
- **因变量：**测量或观察的结果；结果 / 影响
- **控制变量 / 公平实验：**其余条件保持相同，只改变一个因素
- **密度与浮力：**不相溶的液体会分层；密度小 → 上层，密度大 → 下层
